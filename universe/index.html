Controls

Mouse: Left click and drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right click to pan

Keyboard:

  • WASD/QE: Move camera
  • 1-3: Switch realms (Hell/Earth/Heaven)
  • T: Start/stop auto tour
  • P: Pause/resume tour
  • M: Toggle sound
  • F: Toggle fullscreen
  • H: Show/hide help
  • L: Toggle labels
  • R: Reset view
  • , / .: Previous/next destination

About Voxel Universe

An interactive Three.js voxel universe featuring three vertically connected realms: Hell, Earth, and Heaven.

Features:

  • Procedurally generated voxel worlds
  • Animated Cerberus guarding the Gates of Hell
  • Stairway to Heaven connecting Earth and Heaven
  • Saint Peter at the Pearly Gates
  • All nine circles of Dante's Inferno
  • Dynamic time of day system
  • Cinematic auto tour
  • Responsive UI with navigation controls
  • Optional procedural audio

Created with Three.js r152
