Controls & Shortcuts

Camera

  • Left-drag — orbit · Right-drag — pan · Scroll / pinch — zoom
  • Touch — one finger orbit, two fingers pan / pinch-zoom
  • Reset View returns to the current destination

Keyboard

  • 1 / 2 / 3 — jump to Hell / Earth / Heaven overview
  • [ / ] — previous / next destination
  • G — start/stop auto tour · Space — pause/resume tour · N — skip stop
  • T / Y / U / I — Morning / Noon / Dusk / Night
  • L labels · R reduced motion · F fullscreen · M mute · P panel · H help

Special

  • Selecting Cerberus and the Gates of Hell triggers the guardian's roar and the gate opens.
  • Selecting Saint Peter and the Pearly Gates earns his greeting and the gates swing wide.
  • Cerberus heads track your camera as you approach. Beware.
About this world

Voxel Cosmos is a single continuous vertical universe — the nine circles of Dante's Inferno beneath a living human world, and the Kingdom of Heaven above it — built entirely from procedural voxel geometry with Three.js. No textures, no models, no network: every stone, soul, demon, animal, vehicle, angel and flame is generated from a deterministic seed when the page loads.

Descend through the gates Cerberus guards, ride the tour past Charon's ferry and the burning city of Dis, cross the Styx, meet the Dragon at the bottom of the pit — then climb the Stairway, where Saint Peter keeps the Pearly Gates and harps echo between the cloud islands.

Audio is synthesised live with the Web Audio API; it stays off until you ask for it. Tip: lower the quality preset on smaller machines.
