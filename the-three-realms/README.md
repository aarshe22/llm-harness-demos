# The Three Realms

Open `index.html` directly in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox. No server, build step, or Internet connection is needed. The local classic Three.js bundle is in `vendor/three.min.js`; the world, labels, animation, and optional audio are generated by `app.js`.

Drag to orbit, right drag or Shift drag to pan, and scroll or pinch to zoom. Choose destinations from the navigator or use **Auto Tour** for the complete journey. The tour has pause, resume, skip, and stop controls. Sound begins only when enabled.

Shortcuts: **1/2/3** switch realms; **T** starts or stops the tour; **Space** pauses or resumes; **Esc** stops or closes a panel; **←/→** visit previous or next destination; **Q/W/E/R** choose morning, noon, dusk, or night; **M** toggles sound.

World detail uses seeded procedural placement, shared box geometry and materials, instanced vegetation and ground details, pooled animated particles, and adaptive pixel ratio. Low quality reduces particle and cloud density. Reduced motion slows ambient animation.
