VOXEL GOLF

Real 18-hole courses · voxel edition
Course layouts derived from public data: golfMapsR (Google Earth / OpenStreetMap traces, ODbL/MIT). Yardages & pars computed from real geometry. Not affiliated with the courses.

How to play

Touch

  • Drag back from anywhere & release — aim + power (like a slingshot)
  • Two-finger drag: orbit camera · pinch: zoom
  • Tap a club to change · tap Auto for suggestion
  • Buttons at bottom: map, scorecard, camera, drop

Desktop

  • Click-drag back from the ball & release to shoot, or
  • Space (hold & release) to use the power meter
  • A/D or ←/→ fine aim · Q/E club · 1/2/3 shot shape
  • Shift+aim = fast · V camera · M map · Tab scorecard · H help

Rules

  • Water: 1 penalty stroke, drop at the margin
  • Out of bounds / lost: 1 penalty, replay the shot
  • Unplayable lie: use Drop +1
  • Wind affects your shot — check the flag
