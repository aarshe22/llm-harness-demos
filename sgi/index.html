SGI demos — re-implemented in three.js

Faithful-ish WebGL ports of the classic Silicon Graphics demos from sgi-demos.org. Original physics constants, colormaps, geometry tables, the mello electropaint script, the Barcelona radiosity model and the fastobj models are used verbatim; rendering and input are rebuilt on three.js. Each demo links its original source notes in its HUD.

Jello

Thant Tessman · 1987 · exact physics

13-mass icosahedron of springs dropped into a box with a halftone shadow. Same integrator, same dt=0.5.

Logo

Thant Tessman · 1987 · kinematic chain

The SGI cube grown from quads as its tube/elbow joints unfold from 180° to 90°.

Bounce

SGI · 1988 · original .bin models

Three coloured lights riding balls in a lit room; the menu swaps in the real spin/fastobj models.

Electropaint 1988

D. Tristram · Panel Library v7

Four mirrored triangle streams under slow slider LFOs, spectrum colormap, smear & fade.

Electropaint 1989

D. Tristram · mello script

The mature ep driven by Tristram's own script through the ported trapezoidal-oscillator engine.

Electropaint 1994

D. Tristram · OpenGL look

The IRIX screensaver with smooth HLS colour; M toggles the shipped look.

Gview: Barcelona

SGI · radiosity viewer

The original Barcelona.gfo — 2,676 Gouraud polygons with precomputed global illumination. Fly-through.

Newave

SGI · wave propagation

The diamond wave grid with the original spring stencil, speed/grid menus and colour ramp.

Insect

Tessman / Ligon · IK walker

Six-legged walker posed by the original dolegs() IK, flat-shaded from the real parts.c tables.

Ideas in Motion

Thant Tessman · c.1987

Letters assembling on a lit table under a lamp with the SGI logo; track-driven camera.

Flight (1988)

Tarolli / Mace · IRIS 4D tape

The colour-index-era sim: flat-shaded land, meters, takeoff and crash.

Flight 3.4

Mace / Tarolli · IRIX classic

RGB flight with fog, HUD, time of day, and the original hills.grid / hills.t terrain.

Arena

Rob Mace · 1988

First-person mech combat in a colour-index maze — tracers, explosions, a future sport.

Buttonfly

Wade Olsen · demo launcher

The bevelled 3D button menu that tumbles through sub-menus — it launches the ports here.

Cedit

Paul Haeberli · 1984

Pick a colormap entry on the test image, edit it with the R/G/B sliders, live.

Twilight

Howard Look · 1991

The twilight sky gradient with the same 2,700 drand48-seeded stars, star-for-star.

Performer Town

SGI Performer / webfly

A camera tour over a fogged low-poly town — the Performer fly-through in miniature.
